Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to THOP TV. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the THOP TV Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

THOP TV does not collect any personally identifiable information directly. The app does not require account registration or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location data.

Our app may share anonymous, non-personal usage data with third-party advertising partners for the purpose of serving relevant advertisements. This data does not identify you personally.

3. Third-Party Services

The Movie Database (TMDB) — We use the TMDB API to fetch movie and TV show data. Visit TMDB Privacy Policy.

YouTube — Trailers open in the YouTube app or browser. Visit Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob — We may display ads through Google AdMob. Visit Google Privacy Policy.

Meta Audience Network — Ads may be served through Meta's network. Visit Meta Privacy Policy.

AppLovin — Ads may be served through AppLovin MAX. Visit AppLovin Privacy Policy.

Unity Ads — Ads may be served through Unity Ads. Visit Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

THOP TV is free and supported by advertising. Our ad partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalized ads. You can opt out through your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Children's Privacy

THOP TV is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Since we do not store personal data on our servers, there is minimal risk from our side. We encourage you to keep your device software up to date.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the Google Play Store listing for THOP TV.
